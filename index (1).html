Performance
Reimagined

Every Veloce begins as a blank carbon sheet. Our engineers spend over 14,000 hours crafting each vehicle from first principle to final polish — a process that refuses to acknowledge compromise.

The result is not merely a fast car. It is an extension of human ambition — responsive, intuitive, alive.

HYBRID POWER
V12 combustion paired with dual electric motors for instant torque delivery at any speed.
ACTIVE AERO
7-surface adaptive aerodynamic system reacts in milliseconds to driving conditions.
CARBON CELL
62kg aerospace-grade monocoque — stiffer than steel at 40% the weight.
NEURAL DRIVE
AI-assisted chassis system learns and adapts to your driving style within 3 laps.

A Story Written
in Speed & Craft

Founded in 2009 by Luca Ferri and a team of ex-Formula One engineers, Veloce exists to prove that a car can be simultaneously the most beautiful and most capable machine ever made.

The Veloce Manifesto
"We do not build cars for people who drive.
We build cars for people who feel."
LUCA FERRI — FOUNDER & CHIEF DESIGNER
Heritage

Our Journey

2009
FOUNDED IN MODENA
Luca Ferri assembles F1 engineers and master coachbuilders in a repurposed Renaissance factory.
2012
GENEVA DEBUT
The Veloce Prototipo breaks cover — 720 horsepower, carbon monocoque, a roofline that silences the room.
2015
VX-1 PRODUCTION BEGINS
First VX-1 delivered to a Swiss collector. Six-year waiting list forms within weeks.
2019
NÜRBURGRING RECORD
Veloce APEX sets the production car lap record — 6:12.4. A record that still stands.
2024
THE HYBRID ERA
VX-1 goes full hybrid — 1,050 combined horsepower. Zero compromise on character.
